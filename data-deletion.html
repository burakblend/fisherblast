Data Deletion Request

This page explains how users of Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge can request deletion of app-related data managed by Tavook.

App: Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge Package: com.tavook.fisherblast Last Updated: September 13, 2026
Developer identity. Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge is developed and published by Tavook. Tavook Studios is the studio and brand name used for the game and these support/legal pages.

1. What This Page Covers

Fisher Blast uses app-specific cloud records to provide features such as player profiles, custom names, avatars, leaderboards, score synchronization, and related game services. This page provides an external way to request deletion of data managed by Tavook.

2. Account and Identity Note

Fisher Blast does not operate a standalone Tavook email/password registration system. The game may use a Firebase Authentication identity, including an anonymous app identity, and may reconcile supported Google Play Games authentication where available. These identifiers are used to keep game data associated with the correct player profile.

3. How to Request Deletion

Send a deletion request to:

burakakdemir.dev@gmail.com

To help us locate the correct app-managed records without requesting unnecessary personal information, include as much of the following as you know:

  • the app name: Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge;
  • your current Fisher Blast custom or displayed player name, if applicable;
  • your Google Play Games player name, if you used Google Play Games features;
  • your approximate high score or leaderboard position, if known;
  • your approximate last play date;
  • your device model and Fisher Blast game version, if known; and
  • a clear statement that you want your Fisher Blast app-related data deleted.

Please do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment information, or other sensitive credentials.

4. App-Managed Data That May Be Deleted

When we can reasonably identify the correct records, a deletion request may cover Tavook-controlled Fisher Blast data such as:

  • the app-specific Firebase Authentication identity associated with the Fisher Blast profile, where deletion is technically applicable and the identity can be reliably identified;
  • Fisher Blast player-profile records stored in Cloud Firestore;
  • generated, game-service-derived, or custom player-name records stored by Fisher Blast;
  • custom-name change metadata and associated name-registry records;
  • avatar selection;
  • leaderboard/high-score and related synchronization records;
  • app-managed daily-reward or similar synchronization metadata; and
  • support records that can reasonably be linked to the request, where appropriate.

5. Third-Party Data

Some information is controlled directly by third-party providers rather than Tavook. For example, data in your Google account or Google Play Games profile may need to be managed through Google's account, Play Games, advertising, or privacy controls. Deleting Fisher Blast app-managed records does not automatically delete data independently controlled by those providers.

6. What Happens After a Request

We may contact you for limited additional information if needed to locate the correct records or reduce the risk of deleting another player's data. Once the request can be reasonably verified, Tavook will process deletion of applicable app-managed data within a reasonable period, subject to technical, legal, security, fraud-prevention, and recordkeeping requirements.

7. Retention and Exceptions

Some information may be retained where reasonably necessary or legally required for security, fraud or abuse prevention, dispute resolution, legal compliance, platform compliance, or legitimate recordkeeping. Where retention is required, it will be limited to the purpose requiring retention.

8. Contact

For deletion or privacy questions, contact: burakakdemir.dev@gmail.com
