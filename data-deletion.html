Data Deletion Request

This page explains how users of Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge can request deletion of app-related data managed by Tavook.

App: Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge Package: com.tavook.fisherblast Last Updated: September 7, 2026
Developer identity. Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge is developed and published by Tavook. Tavook Studios is the studio and brand name used for the game and these support/legal pages.

1. What This Page Covers

Tavook may manage certain app-related records associated with Fisher Blast, such as leaderboard-related entries, support records, gameplay-related identifiers, or other app data used to operate the game.

2. Account Note

Fisher Blast does not currently offer a standalone username/password account registration system operated directly by Tavook. Depending on how you use the game, app-related data may instead be associated with service-based identifiers, such as Firebase-related identifiers or your Google Play Games profile where you use supported features.

3. How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of app-related data managed by Tavook, please email:

burakakdemir.dev@gmail.com

Please include as much helpful identifying information as you reasonably can, such as:

  • the app name: Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge;
  • your Google Play Games player name, if you used leaderboard or Play Games features;
  • your approximate last play date or the approximate date of your request-related activity;
  • your device model and game version, if known; and
  • a short message explaining that you are requesting deletion of app-related data.

4. Third-Party Data

Some data may be processed or stored directly by third-party services such as Google Play Games or other Google services. Requests relating to data held directly by those providers may need to be managed through your Google account or the relevant service settings.

5. What May Be Deleted

Subject to technical feasibility and applicable obligations, Tavook may delete app-related records such as leaderboard profile information, app-managed identifiers, support records, and other data that can reasonably be linked to your request.

6. Retention and Exceptions

Some information may be retained for a limited period if reasonably necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, platform compliance, or recordkeeping.

7. Contact

For deletion or privacy questions, please contact: burakakdemir.dev@gmail.com
