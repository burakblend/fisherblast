This Privacy Policy explains how Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge handles information when you use the game, profile and leaderboard features, advertising, privacy-choice tools, support channels, and related services.

App: Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge Package: com.tavook.fisherblast Developer: Tavook Last Updated: September 13, 2026
About the developer identity. Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge is developed and published by Tavook. Tavook Studios is the studio and brand name used for the game and these legal/support pages.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge mobile game, its cloud-connected profile and leaderboard features, advertising and consent features, support communications, and associated legal/support web pages.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on how you use the game and which services are available to you, information may include:

  • App identity information, such as a Firebase Authentication user identifier used to associate game data with the correct player profile. The game may use an anonymous Firebase identity and may link or reconcile supported Google Play Games authentication where available.
  • Player profile information, such as a generated player name, a supported game-service-derived display name, a custom player name you choose, your selected avatar, and profile state used by Fisher Blast.
  • Custom-name records, such as the custom name itself, name-change usage information, and a name-registry record used to help keep custom player names unique.
  • Gameplay and leaderboard information, such as high score, score revision or synchronization metadata, ranking-related data, and other records needed to operate the Fisher Blast leaderboard.
  • Reward and synchronization metadata, such as limited timestamps or state used to validate cloud-connected features including daily rewards.
  • Google Play Games information, when you use Google Play Games functionality, such as supported game-service account identifiers or profile information made available to the game for authentication and game features.
  • Advertising and consent information, such as device or advertising identifiers, ad requests, ad impressions/interactions, fraud-prevention signals, and consent or privacy-choice signals processed by Google advertising services where applicable.
  • Technical information processed by the app or integrated service SDKs, such as app version, device or operating-system characteristics, language/region settings, and service diagnostics needed for functionality, security, troubleshooting, or abuse prevention.
  • Support communications, if you contact us, including your email address and any information, screenshots, or device details you choose to provide.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • operate, maintain, secure, and improve the game;
  • authenticate or distinguish player profiles and keep account-scoped game data associated with the correct user;
  • provide profile customization, custom-name uniqueness checks, score synchronization, leaderboards, daily rewards, and other cloud-connected features;
  • display public leaderboard information such as player name, avatar, score, and ranking;
  • serve rewarded or other ads, measure ad delivery, prevent advertising fraud, and honor applicable privacy choices;
  • diagnose technical problems, prevent abuse or cheating, and protect service integrity;
  • respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests; and
  • meet legal, security, platform, and compliance obligations.

4. Firebase Services

Fisher Blast uses Firebase services for app functionality. Firebase Authentication may be used to create or maintain an app-specific identity, including an anonymous identity where appropriate. Cloud Firestore may store app-managed profile and gameplay records such as player names, avatar selection, leaderboard/high-score information, profile state, and related synchronization metadata.

Firebase services are provided by Google and may process technical or service information according to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

5. Google Play Games Services

Where available, Fisher Blast may use Google Play Games Services for game-service authentication and related features. Information made available by Google Play Games may be used to reconcile your game identity and support player-facing game features.

Fisher Blast does not operate a separate email/password registration system. A player profile may instead be associated with an app-specific Firebase identity and, where used, supported Google Play Games authentication.

6. Public Profile and Leaderboard Information

Leaderboard and profile features are designed to show certain information to other players. Depending on your account state and game version, public information may include a generated name, a game-service-derived display name, or a custom player name you choose, together with your selected avatar, score, and ranking information.

Do not choose a custom player name that contains personal information you do not want other players to see. Custom player names may be filtered, rejected, restricted, or moderated to protect users and service integrity.

7. Advertising and Privacy Choices

Fisher Blast uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising, including rewarded advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process device/app information, advertising identifiers or similar identifiers where available, ad delivery and interaction information, fraud-prevention signals, and related data for advertising purposes in accordance with their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

Fisher Blast uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising-related consent and privacy choices where required. In regions where a privacy-options entry point is required, the game may provide a Privacy Choices option in Settings so that available choices can be revisited. The exact options presented may depend on your region, prior choices, and Google's messaging configuration.

8. Third-Party Services

Services used by Fisher Blast may include:

  • Google Play Games Services for supported game-service authentication and features;
  • Firebase Authentication for app identity;
  • Cloud Firestore for app-managed profile, leaderboard, and synchronization records;
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob for advertising; and
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising-related consent and privacy choices where applicable.

These services may process information under their own terms and privacy practices. Their processing may vary by device, region, consent status, service configuration, and the features you use.

9. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Tavook does not sell personal information. Information may be made available to service providers or platform providers when necessary to operate the game, deliver requested features, provide advertising, maintain security, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, or protect users, Tavook, or the public. Public leaderboard information is shared with other players by design as described above.

10. Data Retention

App-managed information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the relevant feature, maintain leaderboard/profile integrity, process support or deletion requests, prevent fraud or abuse, comply with legal or platform obligations, or resolve disputes. Retention periods may differ by data type and service. Third-party providers may apply their own retention practices.

11. Data Deletion and Your Choices

You may request deletion of app-related data managed by Tavook. Depending on what can be reasonably linked to your request, this may include Fisher Blast profile records, custom or generated player-name records, avatar selection, leaderboard/high-score records, name-registry records, app-managed identifiers, and related cloud metadata. See the Data Deletion Request page for instructions.

Data held directly by Google or another third-party provider, such as data controlled through your Google account or Google Play Games settings, may need to be managed through that provider's controls. Advertising choices may also be available through the in-game Privacy Choices entry point where required.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect app-managed information, including authenticated access controls and service-side rules for cloud data. However, no method of electronic transmission, storage, or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Children's Privacy

Fisher Blast is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe information from a child has been provided or processed improperly, contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the game, its features, or legal requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page and the "Last Updated" date will be revised when appropriate.
