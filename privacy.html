Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge handles information when you use the game, leaderboard features, ads, support channels, and related services.

App: Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge Developer: Tavook Last Updated: September 7, 2026
About the developer identity. Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge is developed and published by Tavook. Tavook Studios is the studio and brand name used for the game and these legal/support pages.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Fisher Blast: Ocean Merge mobile game, including app-related support, leaderboard functionality, and associated web pages used for legal and support information.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on how you use the game, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Gameplay and app progress data, such as scores, high scores, basic progress, or related game-state information.
  • Leaderboard-related data, such as your leaderboard score, ranking information, and an in-game display name used to show leaderboard entries.
  • Authentication or account-related identifiers, such as app-related identifiers created through Firebase Authentication or identifiers associated with supported sign-in and game service features.
  • Google Play Games-related information, if you use Google Play Games functionality. For example, your Google Play Games player name may be used as your public display name on the leaderboard where applicable.
  • Device, diagnostics, and technical information, such as device type, operating system information, language settings, app version, crash diagnostics, and performance-related logs.
  • Advertising and consent-related information, including advertising identifiers and consent signals, when ads or ad-consent systems are used.
  • Support communications, if you contact us by email, including the information you include in your message.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information for purposes such as:

  • operating, maintaining, and improving the game;
  • enabling gameplay features, cloud-connected services, and leaderboards;
  • displaying leaderboard names, scores, and rankings to other players where leaderboard features are used;
  • authenticating users and protecting the integrity of app features;
  • serving ads, limiting fraud, and measuring ad performance where advertising is included;
  • debugging technical issues and monitoring reliability, stability, and abuse;
  • responding to support, privacy, or deletion requests; and
  • meeting legal, security, platform, or compliance obligations.

4. Third-Party Services

Fisher Blast may use third-party services that process information according to their own terms and privacy practices. These services may include:

  • Google Play Games Services for game services and related profile/leaderboard functionality;
  • Firebase, such as Firebase Authentication and Firestore, for app functionality, identity handling, and leaderboard-related storage;
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob for advertising; and
  • User Messaging Platform (UMP) or similar consent tools for ad-related privacy choices where required.

These third-party services may independently process information on their own behalf or as service providers, depending on the feature and the applicable service.

5. Public Leaderboard Information

If you use leaderboard features, certain information may be visible to other players. This can include your score, ranking position, and a public display name. Where supported and enabled by the game flow, your Google Play Games player name may be used as the display name shown on the leaderboard.

6. Advertising and Privacy Choices

If the game displays ads, advertising partners may process device and app-related data, including advertising identifiers, ad interaction data, and consent-related information, to deliver ads, limit fraud, and measure performance.

In some regions, users may be shown ad-related privacy or consent messaging, and may also be provided with a way to revisit available ad-privacy choices where required by the applicable advertising framework or platform rules.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose or make information available to service providers, platform services, advertising partners, or legal authorities where necessary to operate the game, provide requested features, prevent abuse, comply with legal requirements, or protect our rights, users, or the public.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including service operation, support, recordkeeping, security, legal compliance, fraud prevention, and dispute handling. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data and the service involved.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. However, no method of transmission, storage, or processing is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children’s Privacy

Fisher Blast is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that information from a child has been improperly provided to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

11. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us regarding privacy questions or to request deletion of app-related data managed by Tavook. More information is available on the Data Deletion Request page.

Please note that some information may be controlled by third-party providers such as Google Play Games or other Google services. Requests involving data held directly by those services may need to be handled through your Google account or the relevant service settings.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and update the "Last Updated" date above.
