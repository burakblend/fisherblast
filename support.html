When Contacting Support

Please include only the information needed to investigate your issue. Helpful details may include:

  • your device model;
  • your Android version;
  • your Fisher Blast game version;
  • your Fisher Blast custom/displayed player name, if the issue concerns your profile;
  • your Google Play Games player name, if the issue concerns Google Play Games or sign-in;
  • your approximate high score, if the issue concerns leaderboard or score synchronization;
  • a clear description of the issue and the steps that caused it; and
  • screenshots or screen recordings, if available and relevant.
Security note. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment details, private keys, or other sensitive credentials to support.

Topics You Can Contact Us About

  • game bugs, freezes, crashes, or device-specific problems;
  • leaderboard, profile, avatar, custom-name, or score synchronization issues;
  • Google Play Games sign-in or game-service issues;
  • rewarded-ad or advertising behavior;
  • privacy choices, privacy questions, or data deletion requests; and
  • general feedback about Fisher Blast.

Privacy and Data Requests

For details about how Fisher Blast handles app-related information, review the Privacy Policy. To request deletion of app-managed data, use the instructions on the Data Deletion Request page.

Response Note

Support requests are reviewed as reasonably possible, but response times may vary depending on request volume, weekends, holidays, or the technical complexity of the issue.
