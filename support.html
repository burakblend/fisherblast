When Contacting Support

Please include as many of the following details as possible:

  • your device model;
  • your operating system version;
  • your game version;
  • your Google Play Games player name, if the issue is related to leaderboard or Play Games features;
  • a clear description of the issue; and
  • screenshots or screen recordings, if available.

Topics You Can Contact Us About

  • game bugs or crashes;
  • leaderboard, profile, or score display issues;
  • support requests related to ads or app behavior;
  • privacy or data deletion questions; and
  • general feedback about the game.

Response Note

Support requests are reviewed as reasonably possible, but response times may vary depending on request volume, weekends, holidays, or the technical complexity of the issue.
